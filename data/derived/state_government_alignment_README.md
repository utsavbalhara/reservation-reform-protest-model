# State government alignment

Party or coalition governing each major state on 2 April 2018 and 21 August 2024 (the two SC/ST Bharat Bandh dates), and
whether it belonged to the coalition governing at the Union level (NDA) on that date. Coded from the public record of state
governments on those dates. Used only as a candidate covariate for policing intensity and deaths (Wilkinson 2004 argues
that state governments' electoral incentives shape policing of communal and caste violence); it is not built into the
model's baseline mechanisms. Andhra Pradesh in April 2018: the TDP left the NDA in March 2018. Jammu and Kashmir in 2024
was a Union Territory administered by the centre.
